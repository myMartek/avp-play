# Controls and controllers

## PlayStation VR2 Sense controllers

Most Quest games expect controllers that are tracked in the room. On the Vision Pro these are the **PlayStation VR2 Sense controllers**. Pair them on the Vision Pro under **Settings › Bluetooth**. The games then pick them up by themselves.

| Sense controller | Becomes in the game |
|---|---|
| Left **Create** button | Menu |
| Right **Options** button | Menu |
| Face buttons | A/B/X/Y as on the Quest |
| **PS button** | belongs to the Vision Pro (Home) – the game doesn't get it |

## Ordinary game controller (DualSense, Xbox)

An ordinary controller works **together with your hands**: your hands show the game where they are and grab with a pinch. The controller adds the sticks and buttons.

| Game controller | Becomes |
|---|---|
| ✕ / ○ / □ / △ | A / B / X / Y |
| L2 / R2 | triggers |
| L1 / R1 | grip buttons |
| Options | menu |

Moss is set up for the game controller. Many other games need real VR controllers, though.

## Surreal Touch

If you have Surreal Touch controllers: in the game's settings choose **Controller Type › Surreal Touch**. You also pair them there (**Discover controllers**) and adjust their direction.

## Hands only

A pinch (thumb and index finger together) works like the trigger. Most games can't be fully played this way, though.

## Quitting and re-centring

- **Quit:** press the Digital Crown. The game ends itself shortly after.
- **Re-centre** (reset your forward direction): press and hold the Digital Crown – as in any other app.

## Performance panel

In any game, hold **Create** and **Options** on the Sense controllers together for **one second**: a small panel appears just above the middle of your view. Do it again and it disappears.

It shows, among other things, frames per second, how busy the graphics chip and processor are, memory and heat. A red bar at a frame-rate drop means: a graphic was just translated for the first time (that only happens once).

Tapping both buttons briefly while the panel is open turns MetalFX on or off (where possible).

## Mixed reality

Some games show your real room around the game: **TRIANGLE STRATEGY**, **Demeo**, **Richie's Plank Experience**, **LES MILLS BODYCOMBAT**, **DreamSpace** and **Cubism**. In the game list, find them with the **Mixed Reality** filter.
