# Terms explained simply

**Xcode** – Apple's free app for developers. AVP Play uses it in the background to build apps for the Vision Pro. You only have to install it and open it once.

**Apple developer team** – your Apple account as Xcode knows it. The games are signed with it. A normal Apple ID is enough (free team); the paid membership removes limits.

**Team ID** – a ten-character code for your developer team. The app reads it from Xcode.

**Signing / signature** – Apple only lets apps run on the Vision Pro that are "signed" by a known account. AVP Play signs every game with your account. With a free account the signature lasts 7 days, with a paid one a year.

**Developer Mode** – a switch on the Vision Pro that allows installing your own apps from the Mac. It appears after the Vision Pro has been paired with Xcode.

**Toolchain** – the translation package. It turns a Quest game into an app for the Vision Pro. Each version has a number (e.g. 620). New versions bring improvements; the app installs them by itself.

**Recipe** – AVP Play's description of a game: which files it needs and their checksums. Verified games have a recipe from the project.

**Job** – an install, download or update. Jobs run one after another and can be resumed.

**Version** – a game's version in the store.

**Purchase source** – where you bought a game: Meta Store or Steam.

**Verified / Community Verified / Untested / Incompatible** – how well a game runs according to tests and reports.

**ovr-platform-util** – Meta's own small sign-in tool. AVP Play needs it to sign in to Meta.

**SteamCMD** – Valve's own tool for signing in and downloading from Steam.

**Rosetta** – a part of macOS that lets programs made for older Intel Macs run on new Macs. Needed for SteamCMD.

**Developer Strap** – an Apple accessory for the Vision Pro with a USB-C port. Copying big games is much faster with it than over Wi-Fi.

**Foveated rendering** – the middle of the picture is drawn sharply, the edges more coarsely. Saves performance and is hardly noticeable.

**MetalFX** – Apple's technique for enlarging a smaller picture sharply. Can make games smoother.

**Mixed reality** – you see your real room around the game.

**Play area** – the space in your room where you can move freely while playing. A see-through wall warns you at the edge.

**Stationary** – playing on the spot or seated, without a measured room.

**Digital Crown** – the dial on top of the Vision Pro. Pressing it quits a game; pressing and holding it re-centres the view.
